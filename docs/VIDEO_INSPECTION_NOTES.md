# Original owner-provided inspiration videos: visual inspection notes

The files in `../assets/` are the **actual three historical user uploads**, recovered and bundled without renaming. Dimensions are 720×1280 at ~30fps. Their content was visually inspected using sampled frames; Claude should review the full animations to judge pacing and transitions.

## 1. inspiration.mp4 (~9.67 seconds)
A fast portfolio inspiration montage seen on a desktop screen. It switches between creative work presentation examples: editorial oversized typography, large portrait/framed images, dramatic high-contrast red/white/black styling, layered floating mockups, composition changes and sharp editorial hierarchy. At approximately 2–8s, examples include bold visual projects/portrait composition and creative services. This does **not** mean the final site should copy the red palette, branded examples, or their personal photos. Relevant design principle: art-directed, recognizable, varied and authentic work presentation rather than identical SaaS cards everywhere.

## 2. inspiration 2.mp4 (~23.54 seconds)
A vertical creator/developer video showing a monitor with a website changing across large editorial sections. Recurring motif: an oversized flowing red paint/brush/ribbon-like cursor trail that crosses or annotates a light website, with very large display headings (`Design`, `Launch` visible in sampled frames) and later substantial visual project panels (`Tidal Audio`, `Sora Skin` in sampled frames). A recurring signal is expressive interaction and visual transformation as a visitor navigates the page; the visual is much more organic than plain particles. For ELALFY: reimagine as tasteful translucent **lime/emerald responsive liquid/filament brush** with plausible lighting, only where useful; do not directly reproduce branded assets, red paths or exact sections. Respect performance/touch/reduced-motion.

## 3. inspiration 3.mp4 (~5.25 seconds)
A clear clip labeled `Glassy Navigation tabs V7`. Contains a floating, rounded glass multi-tab navigation UI with a mix of dark translucent capsules and pale glass segments, high-contrast selected tabs, thin separators and soft luminous highlights; a code/editor window appears under the UI. This is the **main navbar reference**. Build custom responsive ELALFY navigation that borrows glass material, pill geometry, active-state motion and premium feedback—not an exact code copy. On mobile, a simplified functional glass dock/sheet may work better than stacked tabs.
